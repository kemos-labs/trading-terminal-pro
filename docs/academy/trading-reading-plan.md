# How to Start — Learning Trading for Real

A week-by-week path. No gurus. No secret systems. Just mechanics and sources that work.

---

## How to Use This

1. **Do it in order** — each week builds on the last
2. **Read/watch the links** — don't just skim
3. **Write notes** — if you can't explain it simply, you don't know it
4. **Small trades only** — after week 5, put on size you can lose without caring

---

## Week 1 — Mental Guardrails (Before You Look at a Chart)

**Read (45 min total):**

- [Texas Sharpshooter Fallacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_sharpshooter_fallacy) — drawing targets around bullet holes
- [Apophenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apophenia) — seeing patterns in noise

**Exercise:** Next time you think "this setup always works," name which fallacy you're committing. That's the whole week.

## Weeks 2–5 — Auction Theory & Order Flow (Why Price Moves)

Markets are two-sided auctions seeking fair value. Everything else sits on top of this.

### Read First (before any video)
- [TradingRiot — Auction Market Theory](https://tradingriot.com/auction-market-theory/) — cleanest free AMT explanation. Adam Bakay day-traded bonds professionally.

### Watch — MarketLife (Adam Grimes) in Order
Adam Grimes: NYMEX floor trader, hedge fund/prop since 1990s, statistically backtests everything he teaches.

1. [Session 1 — The Trader's Edge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UDJpR0Qy-Qo) — edge, expectancy, why retail chart-reading fails
2. [Session 3 — On Trends](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pNsHTeAClSQ)
3. [Session 5 — Trend/Range Interface](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QtSe_T5dZ40) — **most useful session**: random range vs accumulation/distribution
4. [Session 6 — Practical Templates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NLF7rvljtcc)
5. Continue playlist order — each has PDF slides on his site

### Book (read alongside, don't front-load)
- **Mind Over Markets** — Jim Dalton (classroom-style Market Profile/AMT intro)

### Filter
Skip anything with "secret," "always works," or a branded 3-letter system. AMT is free — never pay for base concepts.

## Weeks 6–12 — Options, Dealer Positioning, Quant Skepticism

Largest block. Maps directly to institutional mechanics.

### Primary Sources First
- [CME Options Institute](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/options-institute.html) — free, structured like a real course, from the exchange
- [CME YouTube — Options Institute webinars](https://www.youtube.com/c/CMEGroup/search?query=Options%20Institute) — skip news clips

### The Source Everyone Paraphrases
- [SqueezeMetrics GEX Research](https://squeezemetrics.com/) — coined "GEX" 2016–17. Dealer gamma hedging *is* the mechanism. Read this before any paid "gamma levels" product.

### Practitioner Accounts (X/Substack)
- [@VolSignals](https://x.com/VolSignals) — career SPX market maker, built VS3D dealer-hedging platform
- [@Gamma_Blast_](https://x.com/Gamma_Blast_) — rates specialist (US/UK/EZ), Substack: sahilm2000.substack.com

### Quant Skepticism (Run in Parallel)
- **Advances in Financial Machine Learning** — Marcos López de Prado. Read Ch.1 + backtest overfitting sections. Antidote to "any TA looks right with enough time."

### Filter
No paid gamma dashboards until you've read SqueezeMetrics yourself. Skip accounts posting GEX charts without explaining the hedging logic.

## Where This Ends & Practice Begins

Reading gets you to: *distinguish real mechanism from curve-fit story.*

It does **not** teach you to trade.

**Next real step (after weeks 1–5):** Put on small, size-controlled trades. Journal the reasoning against the AMT/order-flow framework. Not paper trading indefinitely. Not sizing up before the risk layer (Taleb, de Prado overfitting) has actually changed how you think about position sizing and ruin.
